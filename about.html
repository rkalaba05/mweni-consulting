About Mweni Consulting

An internationally operating consulting firm built on Ubuntu Womanism, an original framework centring the knowledge, leadership and agency of women and girls.

About the Firm

An Internationally Operating Consulting Firm

Mweni Consulting is headquartered in Toronto, Canada, with roots in Zambia. The firm is founded and led by Dr. Racheal Kalaba — the scholar who coined Ubuntu Womanism — and offers a full-range strategic and intellectual portfolio to clients across government, international development, academia, health, civil society, and the private sector.

The firm operates across disciplines and sectors because the challenges it addresses do not respect disciplinary boundaries. Gender injustice shows up in health systems and education policy and community governance and corporate culture simultaneously. Ubuntu Womanism offers a framework that can work across all of these — because it begins not from a sector but from a set of values: relational, collective, grounded, intergenerational, and accountable.

Mweni Consulting is built for the world as it is — complex, cross-contextual, and in need of frameworks that hold across difference. It is also built for the world as it should be: one in which the knowledge of women and girls, of Indigenous communities, of the Global South, shapes the policies and programs that claim to serve them.

Origin of the Name

In the Bemba language of Zambia, mweni speaks to the one who arrives, who is present, who comes with purpose. It carries within it the idea of the guest who brings something essential — not to impose, but to contribute. It is a name for a firm that enters every relationship as a respectful presence, bringing rigour, care, and the light of community-grounded knowledge.

Our Foundation

Ubuntu Womanism: The Intellectual Core

Ubuntu Womanism was coined by Dr. Racheal Kalaba. It is her original theoretical contribution — developed over more than a decade of scholarship, fieldwork, and community practice.

Ubuntu Womanism extends the Southern African philosophy of collective humanity through African feminist and womanist epistemologies, centring the knowledge, leadership, and agency of women and girls as the foundation for transformative and sustainable change. Ubuntu Womanism in Practice
01

Relational Leadership

Leadership is not individual performance. It is the quality of relationships a leader builds, sustains, and is held accountable to.

02

Collective Responsibility

Durable change is change that a community owns, governs, and passes forward. Our work builds collective capacity, not dependence.

03

Spiritual Grounding

Women's knowing is cosmologically situated — connected to ancestors, to land, to the future. This dimension is constitutive, not decorative.

04

Intergenerational Wisdom

Knowledge moves between generations in both directions. Our programs are designed to honour and sustain that flow.

05

Ethical Governance

This firm does not extract, prescribe, or parachute. It earns the right to be in relationship with every community it works alongside.

Board & Governance

Leadership Accountable at Every Scale

Dr. Kalaba's governance roles span national health policy, youth development, international finance, and her own institutions — at every level, she has been accountable not only to boards but to the communities those boards are meant to serve.

Zambia Ministry of Health

Vice Board Chairperson

A national governance role carrying direct accountability for health policy direction, institutional oversight, and the strategic interests of the Zambian people.

Zambia National Youth Development Board

Board Member & Gender/Disability Committee Lead

Leading the Gender and Disability Committee at the intersection of national youth policy, gender equity, and disability rights — governance work that directly informs her approach to inclusive, community-accountable leadership.

ZAMWILL

Founder & Board Member

Founder and governing board member of the Zambia Women Institute of Leadership and Learning, overseeing strategic direction, curriculum integrity, partnership development, and Ubuntu Womanism-centred governance of the organisation's programs across Zambia.

MIJARC — International Rural Youth Movement

World Treasurer (Elected)

Elected World Treasurer of MIJARC, a global social movement spanning multiple continents. A voluntary international governance role carrying financial stewardship responsibilities for a worldwide organisation, and demonstrating the trust placed in her by a global constituency.
