What We Do

Our Services

Eight areas of practice, brought together to fit the full challenge in front of you, for partners at every level of the system.

What We Do

A Portfolio of Capabilities, Not a Fixed Menu

In practice, most engagements draw on several capabilities at once. Clients are invited to bring their full challenge, not only the part they think fits a service category.

Strategic Advisory & Organisational Development

Ubuntu Womanism-grounded consulting for governments, institutions, and NGOs navigating complex change: leadership pipeline development, culture transformation, equity strategy, and accountable governance frameworks.

Policy Development, Advocacy & Legislative Engagement

From community knowledge to national and international policy. Policy briefs, position papers, advocacy frameworks, and legislative submissions for government bodies, multilaterals, and civil society.

Research, Evaluation & Knowledge Production

Community-accountable research and program evaluation using qualitative and mixed methodologies: storytelling circles, photonarrative inquiry, participatory action research, and narrative inquiry.

Curriculum Design, Training & Capacity Building

End-to-end curriculum and training design aligned with UNESCO, ILO, and Bloom's Taxonomy. Programs range from girls' leadership academies to executive development and institutional capacity-building.

Gender Justice & Women's Leadership Programs

Evidence-based program design on gender mainstreaming, women's leadership, and girls' empowerment. Grounded in Ubuntu Womanism and aligned with UNICEF, CAMFED, and Malala Fund Safe Space standards.

Keynote Speaking & Conference Programming

Internationally recognised keynote speaking, plenary presentations, panel facilitation, and conference design on Ubuntu Womanism, women's leadership, gender justice, and transformative education.

International Development & Community Engagement

Community engagement strategy and participatory program design for international development organisations and bilateral agencies across Sub-Saharan Africa, South Asia, the Caribbean, and Indigenous contexts in Canada.

Knowledge Brokering & Public Intellectual Work

Translation of complex research and policy findings into accessible, actionable knowledge for policymakers, funders, community organisations, and the general public.

For Students, Scholars and Institutions

Academic Advising, Grants & Partnerships

Academic Advising & Mentoring

Guidance for undergraduate and graduate students applying to master’s and PhD programmes, support in navigating academic spaces, academic mentoring, and writing and editing.

Grant Writing Support

Support with academic and non-academic grant writing, for individuals and institutions.

Partnerships & Multicultural Training

Support for anyone who wants to work on the African continent, from building partnerships to multicultural training.
